These eight default chip stamps and the four `chip-*` starter cards were copied
from `/home/tyler/pixelqso-chip-cards`. Stamps retain their original exact pixel
dimensions and opaque black pixels. The app installs each bundled stamp once into
the user's stamp shelf, preserving subsequent edits and deletions.

The source pack's WAVs are synthetic E-major tone auditions, not framed modem
waveforms; they are not bundled as transmit audio. Use the app's Listen in feature
to audition cards through its supported modems.
